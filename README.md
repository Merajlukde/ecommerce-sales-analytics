# 🛍️ E-commerce Sales Analytics

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes the Olist Brazilian E-Commerce dataset to understand sales performance, customer behavior, product trends, payment preferences, geographic performance, seller performance, delivery efficiency, and customer satisfaction.

The analysis uses Python and Pandas to transform raw e-commerce data into meaningful business insights and actionable recommendations.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and revenue performance
- Identify top-performing products and categories
- Analyze customer purchasing behavior
- Identify sales trends over time
- Evaluate payment preferences
- Analyze geographic and seller performance
- Measure delivery performance and customer satisfaction
- Generate actionable business recommendations

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Jupyter Notebook
- Data Analysis
- Data Visualization

## 📊 Key Insights

- 💰 Generated approximately **₹1.36 crore in revenue** from **98,666 orders**
- 📦 Average Order Value was approximately **₹137.75**
- 👥 Dataset contains **96,096 unique customers**
- 🔄 Only **3.12% of customers made repeat purchases**
- 🛍️ **Health & Beauty** was the highest-revenue product category
- 💳 **Credit cards** accounted for **73.92% of payment records**
- 📍 **São Paulo (SP)** was the strongest market by order volume and revenue
- 🚚 **89.67% of orders were delivered on time**
- ⭐ Average customer review score was **4.09 / 5**
- 📉 On-time deliveries received an average rating of **4.29**, compared with **2.26** for late deliveries

## 💡 Business Recommendations

1. Improve customer retention through loyalty programs and personalized offers.
2. Focus marketing and inventory planning on high-performing categories.
3. Improve delivery reliability, especially in regions with higher late-delivery rates.
4. Strengthen high-performing geographic markets such as São Paulo, Rio de Janeiro, and Minas Gerais.
5. Use product recommendations and bundles to encourage higher-value purchases.
6. Maintain a reliable credit-card payment and installment experience.

## 📁 Dataset

The project uses the **Olist Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset**, containing information about orders, customers, products, sellers, payments, reviews, and delivery performance.

## 🏁 Conclusion

This analysis demonstrates how e-commerce data can be used to identify growth opportunities, understand customer behavior, evaluate operational performance, and support data-driven business decisions.
